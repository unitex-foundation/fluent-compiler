# Info

This is a partial list of people and companies that have contributed to this project. It is sorted alphabetically by name. Required fields are `name` and `email`, all the rest is added as you desire and must follow this pattern:

```
# as an individual

- **Full Name** (Native Language Full Name, optional) (required)
- nickname: list of nicknames (optional, separated by commas)
- email: list of email addresses (required at least one, separated by commas)
- web: list of web addresses (optional, separated by commas)
- social: list of social links (optional, separated by commas)

# as a company

- **Short Name** (Long Company Name) (required)
- Other Language Short Name (Other Language Long Company Name) (optional)
- web: list of web addresses (required, separated by commas)
- email: list of email addresses (optional, separated by commas)
- social: list of social links (optional, separated by commas)
- phone: list of phone numbers (optional, separated by commas)
- address: legal address (optional)
```

Example:

```
# as an individual

- **John Smith** (Иван Кузнецов)
- nickname: john_smith
- email: john.smith@example.com
- web: [john.smith.example.com](john.smith.example.com)
- social: [telegram](example.com), [discord](example.com), [github](example.com), [gitlab](example.com), [bitbucket](example.com), ...

# as a company
```
